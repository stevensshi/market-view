Warsh's Hawkish Stance at Jackson Hole Reprices Fixed Income Markets · ▼ bear · Warsh hawks repo
Fed Chair Warsh's unexpectedly hawkish speech at Jackson Hole, signaling potential rate hikes and reaffirming the 2% inflation target, drove Treasury yields sharply higher across the curve, with the 2-year yield rising 11.8 bps to 4.348% and the 10-year hitting 4.73%. Mortgage rates followed. The hawkish stance set up a clash with the Trump administration and was praised by the Bank of Korea. However, some longer-duration bonds rallied on relief as the clearer Fed stance reduced uncertainty. Numeric discrepancies exist: another source reported a 5 bps jump in the 2-year to 4.28% and the 30-year easing to 5.19%.
Source: en.sedaily.com
