Uber's Workforce Restructuring Paves Way for Robotaxi Pivot, Stock Climbs · ▲ bull
Uber announced it would cut roughly 10% of its workforce (3,300 jobs) in the largest reduction since the pandemic, flattening management layers and slashing small teams by nearly half. The restructuring is framed as a move to become 'simpler and faster' ahead of major investments in autonomous vehicles and robotaxis. Investors applauded the cost-discipline and strategic clarity, sending Uber stock higher on the day of the announcement.
Source: CNBC
Shein's Disappointing Hong Kong IPO Highlights Valuation Reset for Chinese Cross-Border Retail · ▼ bear
Shein's Hong Kong debut ended flat after plunging as much as 10%, closing at a ~$26B market cap—a 70%+ markdown from its 2022 private valuation of ~$100B. The IPO raised ~$1.7B but was marred by slowing growth, tariff pressures (higher customs duties in Europe and the Middle East), and reputational risks (allegations of forced labor). The weak reception underscores investor skepticism toward Chinese fast-fashion and cross-border e-commerce models amid geopolitical gating and regulatory constraints.
Source: Bloomberg
