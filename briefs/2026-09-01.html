AI Monetization & Productivity Doubts Intensify · ▼ bear · AI Industry
A growing set of evidence challenges the narrative that AI tools are universally boosting productivity and revenue. A METR-funded study found experienced developers using Cursor and Claude were 19% slower (though they felt 20% faster). eMarketer forecasts chatbot ad revenue well below $1B in 2026, sharply lower than OpenAI’s own projections. OpenAI’s deeply negative operating margin (-122%) and stalled ChatGPT user growth, combined with Shein’s poorly received IPO and Korean chip ETF outflows, suggest the AI boom may be facing a reality check on monetization and efficiency gains.
