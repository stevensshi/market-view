Market in one paragraph

RISK-OFF — A global risk-off wave is underway as renewed US-Iran hostilities and tanker attacks in the Strait of Hormuz send oil prices surging, while hawkish central-bank repricing—sparked by Fed Chair Warsh's Jackson Hole speech and euro-zone inflation hitting a near-three-year high—drives bond yields to multi-year highs. Safe-haven flows strengthen the dollar and pressure equities, though AI-infrastructure earnings (Dell, Palo Alto Networks) and chip-boom momentum in Asia provide pockets of resilience. The combination of energy-driven inflation fears and tightening monetary policy creates a stagflationary backdrop, with September seasonality and geopolitical uncertainty compounding the bearish tone.
