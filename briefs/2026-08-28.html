Market in one paragraph

MIXED — Markets are caught between two powerful forces. A blockbuster tech earnings cycle—Nvidia's $400B market cap surge, Salesforce's 22% rally, and record cybersecurity results from CrowdStrike and Okta—is driving a risk-on rotation into AI and software stocks, reinforced by family office bullishness and falling yields. Simultaneously, escalating US-Canada trade war (tariffs, Lake Ontario renaming), persistent geopolitical flashpoints (Strait of Hormuz tanker attacks, CIA Moscow warning, Qatar-Iran mediation), and a hawkish Fed (Hammack calling for rate hikes) are capping risk appetite and keeping safe-haven demand alive. Meta's $17B social-media settlement adds a legal overhang, while a disastrous Himalayan flood deepens human and infrastructure toll. The net picture is a MIXED regime where tech momentum contests with macro and geopolitical headwinds.
