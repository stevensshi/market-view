Volkswagen's supervisory board unanimously approved a sweeping restructuring plan cutting 50,000 additional jobs (bringing total to 100,000 by 2030), halving the model lineup, and ending production at four German plants. The cost-cutting plan was seen as a necessary step to compete with Chinese EV makers and rising costs. VW shares rose on the announcement, and analysts cited a 'halo effect' for the broader German auto sector, lifting names like BMW and Daimler. The bullish narrative focuses on margin improvement and labor compromise.
