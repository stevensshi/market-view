Tape summary
A risk-off week. Doubts over whether massive AI capex will pay off — crystallized by Alphabet's and Tesla's earnings — wiped roughly $767–797bn off the Magnificent Seven, while $100 Brent (Red Sea/Hormuz/Black Sea shipping attacks plus a possible wider US strike on Iran) and a fresh Trump tariff wall on 60 economies revived inflation angst and drove global bonds to 2026-high yields, lifting mortgage rates. A Friday reprieve — Pakistan pushing, with China's backing, to restart US-Iran talks knocked crude ~3% and pulled yields back — plus real bull counters (AI-supercycle dip-buyers, 1969-low jobless claims, a five-month-high euro-zone PMI) kept it from being a clean rout. But leadership, credit and rates all lean the same way into next week's Microsoft/Meta earnings and the FOMC.
